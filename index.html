      How it works

      From a wall of text to a sky of ideas

      Every step below runs on your device. Numbers are live for this galaxy.

        🔒 Your text never leaves this tab. The model is downloaded once from Hugging Face, cached by your browser, and then works offline.

        For students

        Turn a 40-page reading into a study plan

        Built for the moment you open a long chapter, paper or pile of lecture notes and don't know where to start.

        🌙The night before the exam

        Map the chapter, read the Key ideas for a one-minute summary, then Quiz this topic on your weakest topics until they burn gold.

        🔬Reading a research paper

        See every topic at a glance, then Ask the sky “what method did they use?” and jump straight to the answer. No Ctrl+F guessing.

        ✍️Writing an essay

        Connects to shows how two topics link, which is often your argument. Every idea shows its section, so it's easy to cite.

        🗂️Lecture notes all term

        Paste each week's notes, test yourself with Explain it back, and export a checklist Study guide to revise from.
